# Only the user's voice starts a recording

An automatic recording should start only when the user speaks into the microphone. Sound from the speakers (a video, music, a call) and room noise must not start one, because an automatic recording that sits in 録音中 for up to 60 s blocks the user's own dictation until it ends. On 2026-10-26 a video on the Creative T15 Wireless kept the live tray in back-to-back 60 s `MaximumDuration` sessions from 19:34 to 20:25, the speaker gate let a video recording through at 0.318 and 0.530, and the tray crashed with `retained PCM budget exceeded` at 20:34:25. The target design (not built yet) opens the microphone as a Communications stream with the OS acoustic echo canceller referenced to the render endpoint, so everything the speakers play is silence before the VAD sees it. Speaker playback is the main source; another person in the room is not removed by AEC and is left to the speaker gate (`speaker-gate.md`).

## Sub-features

Status 2026-10-26: built. `MicCapture.Open` (`EchoCancelledCapture.cs`) opens the microphone as a Communications stream, points its AEC at the default playback device, and falls back to WinMM with `dictation capture: echo cancellation unavailable, raw input:` when that fails; `dictation.echoCancellation: false` keeps WinMM. The Mac twin is `Listener.start` in `MacApp.swift` (`setVoiceProcessingEnabled(true)`, ducking off); it is compiled-unverified here because this machine has no Mac. In `voice-switch.exe`, `VO1` passes live and fails with the AEC off (recipe below). `VO2` to `VO3` are measured on the probe only. Everything else is not verified yet. Each item names the live log or probe result that passes it.

- `VO1` playback alone starts nothing. With the speakers playing speech for 2 minutes and nobody talking, the input the VAD sees stays at the floor and no `dictation session: automatic` line appears. Probe evidence: the user's own recording played through the T15 for 28 s gave raw -22.4 dBFS and Communications -55.7 dBFS, and the Communications replay started no session (`handoff_wavs=0`) while the raw replay started one and handed off 27 s. A talking-head video at -32 dBFS gave -84 dBFS on the Communications mic through 7 video-only seconds, where the raw mic was at -25 to -31 dBFS.
- `VO2` the user over playback starts a recording and keeps every word. Pass: a session starts within 1 s of the user's first word, and Superwhisper's text from the handed-off audio contains no sentence from the video and drops no sentence that the raw microphone's text has. Probe evidence: 150 s of reading over a talking-head video. The video's interview (in the loopback text) appeared in neither text, and the Communications text had every raw sentence plus one more.
- `VO3` the session ends when the user stops, not at the 60 s cap. Pass: with playback continuing, the session ends `reason=Silence` after the user's last word. Probe evidence: on the Communications replay, sessions covered only the five speaking stretches (8.8-33.6 s, 35.8-58.9 s, 62.1-81.5 s, 100-118.4 s, 131.5 s for 1.2 s), and the raw replay ran two 60 s `MaximumDuration` sessions through video-only time.
- `VO4` the user is never blocked. Pass, over 10 minutes of continuous video with the user dictating three times at random moments: zero `MaximumDuration` sessions without the user's speech, each dictation starts its own session (not appended to a playback session), and no `tray: Error` line. Today's baseline fails all three.
- `VO5` the user alone is unchanged. With nothing playing, own-voice dictation produces the same session lines and the same Superwhisper text as the raw microphone. Covers the Gotcha that a past OS AEC run cut a final 「す」.
- `VO6` a wake word or stop word inside the video does nothing (was `PB1`), and the user's wake word over the video still wakes (was `PB2`).
- `VO7` the AEC follows the render device. Pass: switching the default playback device while listening re-points the reference (a log line naming the new render id, next to today's `dictation loopback: default playback device changed; reopening`), and `VO1` still holds on the new device.
- `VO8` fail-open. A capture endpoint without OS AEC, or `SetEchoCancellationRenderEndpoint` failing, logs one line naming the reason and keeps today's raw behavior.
- `VO9` room noise without speech starts nothing: fan, keyboard typing, a cough. Pass: no `dictation session: automatic` across 2 minutes of each, on the Communications stream (its noise suppressor) with nothing playing.
- `VO10` another person in the room speaking is out of AEC's reach. Expected today: a session starts and `speaker gate: score=` drops it at the end (`SG9`). The goal (only the user starts a recording) needs a speaker check at session start, which does not exist. Track it here so it is not mistaken for covered.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Turn on automatic sessions (`noWakeWord.autoIdle`). Play a video on the speakers. Today a recording starts by itself and the HUD sits in 録音中. With this feature it stays idle until you speak.

## Driving it with verify.sh

`$V aec <name> <seconds> [play.wav]` records three files at once into `/mnt/c/temp/voice-switch-verify/aecprobe/<name>/`: the default capture endpoint raw (`mic-raw`), the same endpoint as a Communications stream with the AEC reference set to the default render endpoint (`mic-comms`), and the render loopback (`loopback`). It copies them into the fixtures as `aec-<name>-raw`, `aec-<name>-comms`, `aec-<name>-loopback` (16 kHz mono PCM16). It pauses the user's installed tray for the recording and starts it again afterwards, printing the tray state; the source is `aecprobe/Program.cs` in this folder, built once into `/mnt/c/temp/voice-switch-verify/aecprobe-bin`. With `play.wav` it plays that file on the default render device; without it, the user plays the video and speaks.

- `VO1`: `$V aec video-only 120` while a talking video plays and nobody speaks, then `$V wav aec-video-only-comms <auto-speaker.json>` and the same for `-raw`. Pass: the comms run has no `dictation session: automatic` line. Unattended variant: `$V aec play-own 30 <fixtures>/own-1792294592.wav`.
- `VO2`, `VO3`: `$V aec talk-over-video 180` while the video plays and the user reads aloud in stretches with pauses. Replay both with `$V wav`, then send the comms handoffs and the three probe files through `$V recall <cases.json>` (cases `[{"name","wav","phrases":[]}]`) and compare the newest `recordings/*/meta.json` `rawResult` texts: loopback text = the video, comms text = the user only.
- `VO5`: `$V aec own-quiet 60` with nothing playing while the user dictates; compare raw and comms text through `recall`.
- `VO9`: `$V aec noise-<kind> 120` per noise, nothing playing, nobody speaking; replay comms with `$V wav`.
- In the app, on the live microphone: pause the tray, run the build with `--config <auto-speaker.json> --dry-run --listen-seconds 45` from `/mnt/c/temp/voice-switch-verify/build` with stdout to an evidence file, play a clip with `powershell.exe -NoProfile -Command "(New-Object System.Media.SoundPlayer '<wav>').PlaySync()"`, then start the tray again. `--output-dir` is refused without `--input-wav`, so `--dry-run` (`dictation handoff: DryRunSuppressed`) is the safe live boundary. `VO1` run 2026-10-26 21:27 with `own-1792294592` (24 s of the user's voice on the T15): the log shows `echo-cancelled 48000 Hz 1ch reference={...4609febf...}`, 3 `speech-onset` lines and no `dictation session: automatic`. The control with `"echoCancellation": false` (`auto-speaker-raw.json`) at 21:28 started `dictation session: automatic` at 151200 and ended `reason=Silence seconds=23.31` with `DryRunSuppressed`.
- `VO4`, `VO6`, `VO7`, `VO10` need the user or a second playback device on the live tray; read `%LOCALAPPDATA%\voice-switch\voice-switch.log`. `VO8` is covered for the config switch by the control above; an endpoint without AEC has not been seen on this PC.

`auto-speaker.json` is `fixtures/auto.json` plus `"speakerEnrollment": "C:\\takeda\\tools\\voice-switch\\speaker.json"`.

## Gotchas

- The probe proves the OS stream, not the app. A pass on `aec-*-comms` through `$V wav` means the AEC output would keep sessions away; the app passes only once it reads that stream itself.
- The Communications stream also runs noise suppression and gain control, and it gates to about -84 dBFS between words. Levels are not comparable with the raw stream, so judge by session lines and transcripts, not by `micDb`.
- The `loopbackDb` and `micOverLoopbackDb` fields on lines from `$V wav` come from the live loopback at replay time, not from the recording. Ignore them in replays.
- `SetEchoCancellationRenderEndpoint` needs Windows build 22621 or later; this PC is 26300 (26H2).
- Pausing the tray and starting it right away can leave it in `State: Error` with `The collection argument is empty and has been marked as complete with regards to additions.` (seen 21:16:30, and at 20:19:31 without the probe). `$V aec` starts it a second time; report the error as a product bug, not a probe failure.
- A video the transcriber cannot read (music, quiet speech) proves nothing for `VO2`. Check that the loopback text has words before you count the comms text as clean.
