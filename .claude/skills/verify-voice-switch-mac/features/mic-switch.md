# Microphone switching, input fallback, and sharing the mic with other apps

The menu bar app's `マイク` submenu pins the listener to one input device, and the choice is kept across restarts. Switching (or resuming on) any listed input must leave the app listening on that device with no alert. Audio from it must reach the recognizer, superwhisper's own toggle key must still record normally while voice-switch listens on the same mic, and wake-word and automatic dictations must work on it. Speaker playback must not start an automatic recording. When the chosen USB or wireless mic is unplugged, the app falls back to the MacBook's built-in mic, and returns when it is plugged back in. This is the only feature in the map that drives the user's installed `VoiceSwitch.app`, because `--simulate` never opens a microphone.

## Sub-features

Each ID is one acceptance check. Run MS1 to MS6 and MS10 for every input in the submenu, at least `MacBook Proのマイク`, one USB mic (`Mic-j5 WebCam JVCU435`, 16 kHz native), `DL-Dock`, and the DJI wireless receiver when it is plugged in.

- **MS1 switch.** Clicking a device in `マイク` logs `listening on <device> at <rate> Hz` and shows no alert. Passed for every direction between the default route and pinned routes (runs `mic-switch-fix4`, `vp-to-pin-c1`, 2026-10-28).
- **MS2 resume.** After `一時停止`, clicking `再開` logs `listening on <device>` and shows no alert. Passed on the built-in fallback and the WebCam (runs `ms2-*`, 2026-10-28).
- **MS3 audio reaches the recognizer.** Within 20 s of speech after MS1, the log has `heard:`, `dictation recognition: complete id=<n>`, or `dictation session: automatic`. A switch that logs `listening on` but never recognizes anything fails. Passed on the WebCam and the built-in mic (run `ms3`). Not judged on the echo-cancelled route with `say`, because the speaker playback is cancelled by design; use a live voice there.
- **MS4 superwhisper's own toggle.** With voice-switch listening on the device, pressing superwhisper's toggle key, speaking, and pressing it again logs `superwhisper direct: recording`, `processing`, then `done`, and superwhisper pastes the text. **Fails today on `MacBook Proのマイク`** (the echo-cancelled route): 4 of 4 tries ended `superwhisper direct: failed` (11:24:30–11:25:30Z, 2026-10-28) and their `output.ogg` measured -91.0 dB mean. The same toggle on the WebCam route passed at -21.6 and -24.8 dB. MS10 is the mechanism. The app no longer uses voice processing (2026-10-28 12:05Z build), so MS10 passes; MS4 itself waits for a run by the user.
- **MS5 wake-word dictation.** Saying `音声入力`, a sentence, then pausing logs `dictation started`, `dictation ended by <ms> ms silence`, `delivery: processing`, `delivery: done`, and the text is pasted.
- **MS6 automatic wake.** Speaking a sentence with no wake word after `inputIdleSeconds` logs `dictation session: automatic rule=auto_b`, then `delivery: done` or `dictation recognition: complete id=<n> rule=auto_b`. It must not end in `dictation dropped: automatic audio had no speech` while the user spoke. Seen passing on the built-in mic with a live voice (11:23:21Z and 11:25:04Z, 2026-10-28).
- **MS7 unplug and replug.** With a USB or wireless mic selected and listening, unplugging it logs `input devices changed, restarting input`, `input device <uid> not found, falling back to MacBook Proのマイク`, and `listening on MacBook Proのマイク` within 10 s, with no alert. Plugging it back logs `listening on <device>`. Implemented, never run (needs a person to unplug).
- **MS8 start with the saved mic missing.** With an unplugged device saved, launching logs `not found, falling back to MacBook Proのマイク` and `listening on MacBook Proのマイク`, not `起動に失敗しました`. Passed with the DJI receiver's UID saved (11:07:41Z, 2026-10-28).
- **MS9 menu lists only microphones.** The submenu holds `システムのデフォルト` and real inputs only, also while voice processing runs. Passed (runs `mic-switch-fix4`, `menu-fix`).
- **MS10 other apps never get silence.** While voice-switch listens on a route, another process opening the same mic raw gets the same channel count as without voice-switch, a downmix with no all-zero buffers, and a level within 6 dB of its baseline. **Fails today on the echo-cancelled route**: with an AVAudioEngine voice processing engine holding the built-in mic, a raw reader saw the mic turn from 1 ch to 3 ch, each channel dropped from -38.9 to about -82 dB median, and the mono downmix was all zeros (80 of 80 buffers). With HAL voice activity detection on the same mic instead, the reader stayed at 1 ch and -38.1 dB. That design shipped: with the installed app listening on the built-in mic, the reader saw 1 ch, -39.4 dB median, and no zero buffers (11:52Z), and 1 ch with 0 of 80 zero buffers on the final build (12:05Z).
- **MS11 playback does not start a recording.** Playing speech from the default output (the speakers) while listening never logs `dictation session: automatic`. This is what echo cancellation is for. The app now starts an automatic recording only after the input's HAL voice activity detection has heard one voice for `Listener.minVoiceSeconds` (1.5 s); otherwise it logs `automatic wake blocked: no voice activity (speaker playback is not a voice)`. Without that minimum, `say` from the speakers still started a session in 2 of 4 runs, because the detector blipped on for 725–1236 ms at the start of playback in 3 of 6 runs. With it, 6 of 6 speaker runs started nothing (12:00–12:02Z, 2026-10-28).
- **MS12 a voice that is not playback is detected.** Speech from a source outside the echo reference (a live voice, or `say -a <non-default output id>` as a stand-in) is detected. With HAL voice activity detection on the built-in mic it was on for 161 of 240 polls, twice, while speaker playback gave 0 of 240, twice. In the app, speech from the DL-Dock output held it on for 8000 ms, but never reached the automatic-recording check (no `automatic wake blocked` or session line), probably because it is too quiet at the mic for the segmenter (unverified). So the in-app MS12 proof is MS6 with a live voice. Keep the DL-Dock output volume low when using it as a source; the user asked for it to be turned down.

- **MS13 the first sentence survives the voice activity gate.** Saying two sentences with a short pause and no wake word (`昔々あるところに、おじいさんとおばあさんが住んでいました。おじいさんは山へしばかりに…`) hands both to superwhisper. When the gate held the first one back, the log has `automatic wake blocked: no voice activity`, then `dictation session: automatic keeps <ms> ms held for voice activity` before `dictation session: automatic`, and the superwhisper result starts with `昔々`. Before the fix, sessions at 12:18:29Z and 12:19:17Z (2026-10-28) began at the second sentence (`おじいさんは山へ…`) while the received `output.ogg` had no leading silence, so voice-switch, not superwhisper, dropped it.

- **MS14 the HUD answers a voice before the gate opens.** With no wake word, the HUD shows the mic glyph (`hud: waiting phase=hearing`) as soon as voice activity detection hears the user, then the red dot (`hud: recording phase=recording`) once the automatic session opens, so the user does not wonder whether the mic is listening during the 1.5 s gate. Speaker playback shows nothing: 3 of 3 runs logged no `hud:` line (12:35:55–12:36:23Z, 2026-10-28). superwhisper's own recording keeps its red dot while the user speaks into it (`hearing` ranks below every delivery). Every HUD change is logged as `hud: <glyph> phase=<phase> delivery=<state>`, so a HUD the user reports missing can be traced in `~/Library/Logs/voice-switch.log`.

- **MS15 a mic glyph the user speaks into turns into a recording.** On every input, a live sentence of about 3 s with no wake word goes `hud: waiting phase=hearing` → `dictation session: automatic rule=auto_b` → `hud: recording phase=recording` within 2 s of the first `hearing` line. `hearing` followed by `hud: hidden phase=idle` while the user is still speaking fails. Run it with `mic-holder vad` beside the app (see Driving) and quote `longest-on-ms` for the same sentence. **Fails today on `MacBook Proのマイク`** (build of 37256c9, installed 2026-10-28 22:38Z): from 22:38:37Z to 23:38Z the log has 195 `hearing` → `hidden` with no session, 180 of them within 1 s, while the 26 that opened a session stayed `hearing` 2 to 7 s (23:34:34–23:34:41Z: four 1 s flashes in a row, then a session at 23:34:53Z). Mechanism, from source and unverified live: the glyph follows `voiceBeingHeard()` (HAL detector on at this instant, `MacApp.swift:999`), but the session needs `voiceRecentlyActive()` (`MacApp.swift:877`), one unbroken on-run of `Listener.minVoiceSeconds` (1.5 s). Every off transition clears `onSince`, and `lastVoiceEnd` is set only after a run of 1.5 s (`MacApp.swift:848–851`), so a detector that drops out for a moment between words never opens the gate however long the user talks. The block reason stays `no voice activity`, which is logged only on a change, so these misses leave no `automatic wake blocked` line. A fix passes when the sentence opens a session while speaker playback still opens none (MS11).
- **MS16 the stop word ends an automatic recording on every input.** With no wake word, a live sentence followed by each configured stop word the user says (`入力ストップ`, and bare `ストップ`, which `config.stop-auto.json` does not list) logs `dictation finished by stop word`, `end reason=StandaloneStop`, and `automatic ended by StandaloneStop, pasting like a wake-word dictation`, and the pasted text does not end with the stop word. `end reason=Silence` or the finish hotkey (`FinishCommand`) after the stop word was said fails. **Fails today on `MacBook Proのマイク`**: the same build stopped once at 22:50:04Z (session of 6.84 s), and after the relaunch at 22:50:24Z 0 of 14 automatic sessions ended by the stop word (11 `Silence`, 3 `FinishCommand`) while the user reported saying it. Cause not found. The stop check runs only on a segmenter `.utterance` or `.tail` event (`MacApp.swift:1063–1074`), while the session stays open on `speaking`, which also counts the HAL detector (`MacApp.swift:1013`), so speech under the energy threshold keeps recording but is never checked. That is one candidate, unverified. A transcript clipped to `入力ストッ` is another (stop-word.md). The log cannot tell them apart: with `noWakeWord` on, `stopWordCut` logs nothing (`dictating:` is suppressed), so a stop check that ran and missed looks the same as none at all. Until the app logs the check's outcome without the text, judge MS16 by the end reason only, and say the cause stays unknown. The app now logs it (`stop check:` below). With `echoCancellation: false` on the built-in mic, the stop word ended 6 sessions between 23:58Z and 00:34Z (2026-10-28/29). The two at 00:34:04Z and 00:34:17Z ran on the logging build and follow `stop check: kind=utterance … matched=yes`.

- **MS17 the built-in mic hears a normal voice with `echoCancellation: false`.** This is the acceptance check for the planned fix: `false` turns off only the 1.5 s gate, and the HAL detector keeps counting as voice. superwhisper's own toggle records the same voice fine, but it has no voice gate and its saved audio is normalized to about -1 dB peak, so it is no evidence of the level voice-switch reads. **Fails today** on `MacBook Proのマイク` (00:31–00:35Z, 2026-10-29). Of 23 `voice: phrase` lines, 17 ended `-> shorter than minSpeechMs`, the longest had `voicedMs=750`, every line had `halMs=0`, and `peakRms` had a median of 0.0122 against a `threshold` median of 0.0082 (noise floor about 0.0027 times `vadRatio` 3). The same sentences opened sessions at once on `Mic-j5 WebCam JVCU435`. Raising the voice gain by config (`vadRatio`) was rejected as flaky, because the margin moves with the room noise. Run each check on the built-in mic and on the WebCam, five sentences of about 3 s each, with no wake word:
  - `MS17a detector on.` After `listening on <device>`, one line says the HAL detector is on and the gate is off for that device. The line is new with the fix; with `echoCancellation: true` it says the gate is on.
  - `MS17b voice reaches the gate.` Each sentence gives one `voice: phrase … -> session opened` line with `halMs` above 0. `-> shorter than minSpeechMs` with `halMs=0` while the user spoke fails. Quote `voicedMs`, `energyMs`, `halMs`, `peakRms`, and `threshold` per sentence, so a pass shows which source carried it.
  - `MS17c no gate.` No `automatic wake blocked: no voice activity` line, and no `voice: phrase … -> automatic wake blocked: no voice activity`, while `echoCancellation` is `false`.
  - `MS17d HUD.` Each session goes `hud: waiting phase=hearing`, then `dictation session: automatic confirmed by agreed words`, then `hud: recording phase=recording`. A `hud: waiting phase=hearing` followed by `hud: hidden` with no `session opened` while the user still speaks fails (the MS15 failure).
  - `MS17e stop word.` For a sentence ending in `入力ストップ` and one ending in bare `ストップ`, the log has `stop check: … matched=yes`, `dictation finished by stop word`, and `end reason=StandaloneStop`. `stop check: … matched=no` on the utterance that held the stop word, or `end reason=Silence`, fails (the MS16 failure). `recognizer=failed` is a recognizer failure, reported apart.
  - `MS17f handoff.` Each delivered session has, in order, `superwhisper handoff: open audio=<s>s mode=<m>`, `superwhisper handoff: open exited status=0`, `superwhisper run: started id=<run>`, `superwhisper run: result id=<run> kind=<kind>`, and `dictation: <n> chars`. Any `superwhisper handoff: not dispatched, <reason>` is quoted with its reason. `started` with no `result` means superwhisper stalled; no `started` means it never took the file.
  - `MS17g echoCancellation true unchanged.` With `true`, MS11 still passes (speaker playback opens nothing and logs `automatic wake blocked: no voice activity`), and MS17a says the gate is on.
  - `MS17h playback with false.` Play `say` from the speakers for 30 s with `false`. Report how many `voice: phrase … -> session opened` lines it causes and whether each was dropped later (`had no speech`, `had no agreed words`, `another speaker`). This is the known cost of `false`; record it, do not fail on it.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Click the menu bar icon, open `マイク`, and click a device. `一時停止` and `再開` are the first item of the same menu.
- superwhisper's toggle key is the user's own superwhisper shortcut.
- Unplug or replug the USB cable or the wireless receiver.

## Driving it with verify.sh

Preconditions:

- The user agreed to driving their installed app. It changes the saved mic, may pop an alert, and hands dictations to the real superwhisper (MS4 to MS6).
- The build under test is installed with `make relaunch`. This is the one recipe that uses `make`, because the menu exists only in the installed app. `$V build` does not cover it.
- The Terminal running the helper has Accessibility permission (System Events clicks the menu).

- **MS1, MS2, MS3.** Run `VS_RUN_ID=<id> [VS_SPEAK="<Japanese text>"] .claude/skills/verify-voice-switch-mac/mic-switch.sh [device title ...]`. With no titles it walks every submenu item. It resumes first when the app is paused, opens `マイク`, clicks each device, waits `VS_SWITCH_WAIT` seconds (default 4), reads any alert and presses OK, and prints `PASS <device>: listening on ...` or `FAIL <device>: <alert>`. With `VS_SPEAK` it plays the text with `say` after each passing switch and prints `PASS <device> audio:` when a recognition line follows (MS3). Then it restores the previously selected device and the paused state. It exits 1 on any FAIL. Evidence goes to `~/Library/Caches/voice-switch-verify/evidence/<id>/mic-switch/` (`menu-items.txt`, `state-before.txt`, `targets.txt`, and `results.txt` with the log lines and alert text per switch).
- **MS10.** Compile once: `swiftc -O .claude/skills/verify-voice-switch-mac/mic-reader.swift -o "$TMPDIR/mic-reader"`. Find device ids with `say -a '?'` (outputs) and the app log (inputs), or `system_profiler SPAudioDataType`. Run `"$TMPDIR/mic-reader" 8 <input id>` once with voice-switch on a pinned route (baseline) and once with it on the route under test, while the same `say` plays. It prints the format, per-channel and downmix levels, and the count of all-zero buffers. Compare against the MS10 limits. To test a candidate design without the app, `swiftc -O .claude/skills/verify-voice-switch-mac/mic-holder.swift -o "$TMPDIR/mic-holder"` and run `"$TMPDIR/mic-holder" vpio <seconds>` or `"$TMPDIR/mic-holder" vad <seconds> <input id>` beside the reader.
- **MS11, MS12.** With `mic-holder vad <seconds> <input id>` running, play `say -a <default output id>` (MS11) and `say -a <other output id>` (MS12). It prints `polled-on=<on>/<polls> longest-on-ms=<ms>` at 50 ms per poll. `longest-on-ms` is the longest unbroken on-run; with `echoCancellation: true`, a spoken sentence under 1500 ms there cannot pass the app's voice activity gate (MS15). With `false` the gate does not apply. In the app, MS11 is the absence of `dictation session: automatic` while `say` plays from the speakers.
- **MS17.** Needs the user's live voice, a fix build installed with `make relaunch`, and `"echoCancellation": false` in `~/.config/voice-switch/config.json`, followed by `一時停止` then `再開` (the detector is set up only when listening starts). Hold the log offset, ask for the five sentences per mic, then filter `~/Library/Logs/voice-switch.log` for `listening on`, `voice: phrase`, `automatic wake blocked`, `hud:`, `dictation session:`, `stop check:`, `dictation finished`, `superwhisper handoff:`, `superwhisper run:`, and `dictation:`. Count per sentence; do not judge by the user's impression of the HUD alone. Before the live run, `.claude/skills/verify-voice-switch-mac/segmenter-voice/run.sh` proves offline that a frame the detector hears is speech to the Segmenter (utterance, head, tail, no floor drift) even below the energy threshold, and that without the detector it stays energy only; it must end `segmenter-voice: ok`.
- **MS15, MS16.** Need the user's live voice and paste into the front app, so ask first and put a scratch TextEdit document in front. Hold the log offset, ask the user for five tries each (a 3 s sentence for MS15; a sentence then `入力ストップ`, and a sentence then `ストップ`, for MS16), and count the lines named above per try. For MS15 run `"$TMPDIR/mic-holder" vad 30 <input id>` during the tries.
- **MS4 to MS7.** These need a person to speak or unplug. Ask the user to do each step on each device while you hold the log offset, then quote the lines named above. For MS4 also measure the newest `~/Documents/superwhisper/recordings/<id>/output.ogg` with `ffmpeg -nostats -i <file> -af volumedetect -f null -` (silence is about -91 dB mean).

## Gotchas

- With voice processing on, `kAudioOutputUnitProperty_CurrentDevice` on the input node's unit names the output device (`MacBook Proのスピーカー`, id 76), and voice processing always captures the system default input. Together with MS10, that is why the app no longer uses voice processing at all: every route is raw input, and HAL voice activity detection gates automatic recordings instead. Before this, writing the mic into that property gave `input format 0.0 Hz, 0 ch` (the original MS1, MS2, and MS8 failures, 2026-10-28 10:10–10:48Z).
- While voice processing runs, every output device grows an echo-reference input stream (terminal type `spkr` or 0) and an aggregate device appears. Pinning one of those fails with -10851. The menu lists inputs by stream terminal type for that reason.
- With voice processing on, the built-in mic reports 9 identical channels to its owner and 3 channels to other processes, and a mono downmix of either is all zeros. voice-switch reads channel 0 only. Other apps that downmix get silence (MS10).
- HAL voice activity detection (`kAudioDevicePropertyVoiceActivityDetectionEnable` / `State`) runs only while some client does input IO on the device. Its property listener fired once per transition in these runs, so poll the state or keep the last value. On the USB WebCam it detected the non-reference voice (157 of 240) but also some speaker playback (14 of 240). The DL-Dock input gave 0 for both, probably because nothing is connected to it (unverified).
- A click from a separate `osascript` after another call opened the menu does nothing, and `ignoring application responses` drops the click too. A click on a submenu item without opening `マイク` first can land on the next item (`設定ファイルを開く`). `mic-switch.sh` opens the menu and `マイク` and clicks in one call under `with timeout of 5 seconds`.
- A failed `再開` leaves the listener stopped, so later device clicks only save the choice and log nothing. The helper stops after a failed resume.
