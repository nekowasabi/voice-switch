# Automatic sessions without a wake word

With `dictation.noWakeWord` switched on, speech can open a dictation without the wake word. Three ways exist. `followup` keeps listening for `followupMs` after a dictation. `autoIdle` starts on speech; the target behavior (issue "自動ウェイクを操作中でも発火させる") no longer requires the keyboard and mouse to be idle; `inputIdleSeconds` is only logged. `relaxedReadingDistance2` accepts a reading close to a wake word. All three require `dictation.superwhisperMode` to be set, and the recording goes only to history, never pasted. An automatic recording records from its first sound, but the recording HUD (`phase: recording`) appears only once Apple Speech and Whisper agree on words in it. The check runs on the audio so far after every further second, and a confirmed recording logs `dictation session: automatic confirmed by agreed words` and skips the agreement check at handoff. An unconfirmed one is checked at handoff instead (`dictation agreement: shared=<n> ms=<latency>`), after `minHandoffMs` and Silero.

## Sub-features

- `auto-idle` logs `dictation session: automatic rule=auto_b` after the keyboard and mouse were idle. The result goes to Superwhisper's no-auto-paste mode.
- `auto-busy` (implemented by issue #33; `d3685d6` and earlier fail it). Speech while the user is operating the Mac also logs `dictation session: automatic rule=auto_b inputIdleSeconds=<below 1>`. Checks:
  - `AB1` the session starts at all (the baseline does not, see Gotchas).
  - `AB2` the recording starts at the phrase head, not at the last input. The session's audio length on `dictation session: end ... seconds=` is close to the spoken phrase (the `busy-speech` phrase is about 2 s), not near zero.
  - `AB3` the recording reaches Superwhisper even while operating. The log shows `superwhisper mode: <mode>` and a `dictation:` result line (or `dictation delivered`) after the `end` line. A session that only logs `dictation recognition: complete` went to local history and fails AB3 (the 2026-10-14 regression of the first #33 build).
  - `AB4` focus comes back. Superwhisper may take the front while opening the file; `busy-input.txt` must show the previous app frontmost again within 2 s of `com.superduper.superwhisper`.
  - `AB5` a wake word spoken while operating still wins. `音声入力 今日は晴れです` under busy input logs `automatic replaced by wake word` and a normal dictation.
- `auto-followup` logs `rule=followup` within `followupMs` of a finished dictation.
- `auto-relaxed` logs `dictation session: wake-only rule=relaxed_d2`.
- `auto-short-dropped` drops an automatic recording shorter than `minHandoffMs` (default 1000). A cough or a click logs `dictation dropped: automatic audio shorter than minHandoffMs` and never reaches Superwhisper. Drive it with `$V say cough2 "[[slnc 1500]] えへん [[slnc 3000]]"` and `SIMULATE_REALTIME=1 $V run cough2 config.stop-auto.json` while nobody touches the keyboard or mouse. Under busy input the same drop must still happen (`AB6`).

- `auto-no-speech-dropped` (issue #35) drops an automatic recording long enough to pass `minHandoffMs` but holding no voice. Silero VAD scores no 32 ms window at 0.5 or above, so it logs `dictation dropped: automatic audio had no speech` and never reaches Superwhisper. `say` cannot make noise, so write the `noise-burst` fixture yourself into `~/Library/Caches/voice-switch-verify/fixtures/noise-burst.aiff`: 16 kHz mono, 1.5 s quiet, 2 s of decaying clicks (a keyboard), 3 s quiet. Run it like `busy-speech`. Expect `dictation session: end ... seconds=<about 2>` then the `had no speech` line, and no `superwhisper mode:` line. `busy-speech` is the counterpart that must still reach Superwhisper. Proven on 2026-10-14 (runs `silero-noise`, `silero-speech`).
- `auto-cough-dropped` drops a voiced cough that Silero lets through. Apple Speech and Whisper transcribe the recording, and fewer than 3 shared characters logs `dictation agreement: shared=<n> ms=<latency>` then `dictation dropped: automatic audio had no agreed words`, with no `superwhisper mode:` line. `say` cannot cough and the repo is public, so bring your own recording. Copy a cough out of `~/Documents/superwhisper/recordings/<id>/output.wav` (one superwhisper turned into 「あっ!」 or 「よし!」) and run `afconvert -f AIFF -d BEI16@16000 -c 1 <wav> ~/Library/Caches/voice-switch-verify/fixtures/cough-real.aiff`, then `SIMULATE_REALTIME=1 $V run cough-real config.stop-auto.json`. Check the gate first with `voice-switch --check <wav>`; its stderr line `agreed=` must be below 3. `busy-speech` must log `dictation session: automatic confirmed by agreed words` (or, if it ends before a check agrees, `dictation agreement: shared=` of 3 or more) and still reach Superwhisper. A confirmed recording logs no `dictation agreement:` line. The Whisper model must be in place (`make model`); without it the app logs `whisper: unavailable` and the check is skipped. Proven on 2026-10-14 (run `agree`: noise-burst dropped by Silero, two real coughs `shared=1` and `shared=0` dropped, busy-speech `shared=12` delivered, about 0.4 s added).

- `auto-hud-after-agreement` keeps the recording HUD hidden until the words agree, so a cough or a noise never flashes it, while real speech shows it about a second after it starts, long continuous speech included. Proof is the order of `dictation session: automatic confirmed by agreed words` then `phase: recording`; a dropped cough or noise shows no `phase: recording` line at all. Drive `cough-real`, `noise-burst`, `busy-speech`, and a long phrase (`$V say long-speech "[[slnc 1500]] 今日は午前中に資料をまとめて、午後からは会議の準備を進めて、夕方までに全員へ共有する予定です [[slnc 3000]]"`) with `SIMULATE_REALTIME=1`. Proven on 2026-10-14 (run `hud2`: long-speech confirmed about 1 s after start, coughs and noise never showed the HUD).
- `auto-short-then-speech` covers a short sound and real speech in one file: `$V say mixed "[[slnc 1500]] えへん [[slnc 3500]] 今日の会議は三時からです [[slnc 3000]]"` and `SIMULATE_REALTIME=1 $V run mixed config.stop-auto.json`. Expect the first session to end with `automatic audio shorter than minHandoffMs` and the second to reach `superwhisper mode: input`. Kyoko's えへん is a clearly spoken word, so it gets `confirmed` and shows `phase: recording` for under a second before `minHandoffMs` drops it; a real cough does not.

- `auto-stop-pastes` hands an automatic recording the user ended with the stop word off like a wake-word dictation: no switch to the no-paste mode, and Superwhisper pastes into the app in front. One that ended on silence stays history-only. Put a scratch TextEdit document in front (`osascript -e 'tell application "TextEdit" to activate' -e 'tell application "TextEdit" to make new document'`), never the terminal, then run `long-tail` and `busy-speech` with `SIMULATE_REALTIME=1` and read it back with `osascript -e 'tell application "TextEdit" to get text of front document'`. Expect `long-tail` to log `automatic ended by stop word, pasting like a wake-word dictation`, no `superwhisper mode:` line, `dictation delivered: superwhisper`, and its sentence (without 入力ストップ) in TextEdit; `busy-speech` logs `superwhisper mode: input` and adds nothing. Close the document with `saving no` afterwards. Proven on 2026-10-14 (run `paste`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Idle path. Stop typing for a second or more, then speak.
- Busy path. Keep typing or moving the mouse and talk at the same time.

## Driving it with verify.sh

Preconditions:

- The user agreed to a run that may drive their Superwhisper and switch its mode. The busy path must not touch Superwhisper; if it does, that is the failure being shown.
- Config `config.stop-auto.json` in this folder (`superwhisperMode: input`, `autoIdle: true`), or a scratchpad copy of it.

- **Idle start.** Do not touch the keyboard or mouse. Run `$V say auto-idle "今日は晴れです [[slnc 2000]]"` and `VS_RUN_ID=<id> $V run auto-idle <config>`. The output has `dictation session: automatic rule=auto_b inputIdleSeconds=<n>` with n of at least 1.
- **Busy start (AB1 to AB4).** `$V say busy-speech "[[slnc 1500]] 今日の会議は三時からです [[slnc 3000]]"`, then `VS_BUSY_INPUT=1 SIMULATE_REALTIME=1 VS_RUN_ID=<id> $V run busy-speech config.stop-auto.json`. `VS_BUSY_INPUT=1` runs `busy-input.swift`, which posts an in-place mouse move every 200 ms for 45 s and samples the frontmost app. Expect, in order: `dictation session: automatic rule=auto_b inputIdleSeconds=0.<n>`, `dictation session: end ... seconds=<about 2>`, `superwhisper mode: input`, a `dictation:` result line, and in `busy-input.txt` no `com.superduper.superwhisper` entry left frontmost for more than 2 s.
- **Busy wake word (AB5).** `$V say busy-wake "[[slnc 1500]] 音声入力 今日は晴れです [[slnc 3000]]"` and run it the same way. Expect `automatic replaced by wake word`.
- **Busy cough (AB6).** Run `cough2` with `VS_BUSY_INPUT=1`. Expect `dictation dropped: automatic audio shorter than minHandoffMs`.

## Gotchas

- `busy-input.swift` needs the terminal running `verify.sh` to hold the Accessibility grant. Without it every posted move is dropped, the session logs `inputIdleSeconds` of many seconds, and the run proves nothing about the busy path. `$V doctor` warns about it, and the helper writes `busy-input: FAILED` into `busy-input.txt`. The 2026-10-14 runs `silero-noise` and `silero-speech` hit this (`inputIdleSeconds=8.148` and `60.803`).

- With `noWakeWord` enabled the `heard:` lines are suppressed for privacy. Read the `dictation session:` and `dictation recognition:` lines instead.
- Missing `superwhisperMode` silently disables all three. A run with no `rule=auto_b` line should first check the config.
- Without `VS_BUSY_INPUT`, your own typing during the run changes `inputIdleSeconds`. Do not type during idle-path runs.
- Baseline on `d3685d6` (2026-10-14, run `busy-baseline`): busy input gives only `dictation recognition: complete id=1 rule=strict inputIdleSeconds=0.007` and no `automatic` line. That is the user's complaint, reproduced. A passing `auto-busy` run must differ from it.
- `AB4` counts every frontmost change, including the user's own app switches. The 2026-10-14 baseline logged 7 changes, all from the person at the keyboard. Ask the user to leave the Mac alone during the run, or judge AB4 only by changes to `com.superduper.superwhisper` or any change within 2 s after the `end` line.
- The first #33 build routed busy sessions to local history (`route=local`) to avoid the focus steal, so Superwhisper never ran while the user worked. That design was dropped; do not reintroduce a busy-only local route.
- Synthetic Kyoko audio has no keyboard noise, so these runs do not show whether key clicks leak into the recording. That needs a live check with real typing.
